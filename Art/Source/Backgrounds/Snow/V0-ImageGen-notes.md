# Snow V0 ImageGen source notes

The three extraction files were created with built-in ImageGen in generation mode, using `V0-Day-source.png` as the composition reference. They are intentionally high-resolution source plates; `Tools/New-SnowBackgroundPrototype.ps1` removes the preview checkerboard, applies nearest-neighbor scaling, limits each palette, seals repeat edges, and emits the exact production dimensions.

- **Far:** pale blue-white frozen mountain skyline with a ruined prewar research city, broken wind/transmission towers, distant domes and antenna structures, broad uninterrupted horizontal panorama, no foreground objects.
- **Mid:** darker frozen industrial ruins with a left control tower, exposed pipeline, collapsed half-buried central bunker with restrained amber heat windows, and a right antenna platform; readable silhouettes with open sky between structures.
- **Close:** darkest near-camera snow banks, dead conifers and broken poles concentrated toward the sides, sparse cables and debris, with a broad open center that does not obscure the player.

The production target is original Terraria-compatible pixel art: hard edges, compact palettes, no text or logos, no characters, no UI, and no copied game assets.
