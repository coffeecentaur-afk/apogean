# Upper-building style pass

Built-in image-generation edit, one candidate per building. Gas station and garage are references only, not edit targets. No in-game installation in this pass.

## BrokenShell

Use case: style-transfer. Asset: Terraria Wastes midground BUILDING TOP ONLY.
Input1 is the edit target, BrokenShell; Input2 is the APPROVED gas station and Input3 the APPROVED garage. Inputs2/3 are material-drawing references ONLY: do not redraw them or include them in the output.
Primary request: redraw ONLY Input1's building materials in the pixel language of Inputs2/3. It currently looks too finely stippled and realistically rendered. Preserve its recognizable architecture, footprint, height, facing, lighting direction, and short ground footing. Keep the two-storey broken rectangular facade, missing right-hand roof and wall, empty dark windows, exposed floor slab, and short footing. Approximately 320 pixels wide at final 512x460 display scale, about 210 pixels tall above its ground.
Drawing rule: broader readable stepped pixel clusters like the references' paint chips and garage wall panels. Use large simple shadow planes; thick dark door/window recesses; a few deliberate angular crack groups; grouped missing plaster exposing burnt-red undercoat, dirty warm beige, smoky gray and rusty metal. Damage stays rough and obvious but grouped, NOT hundreds of tiny light freckles. Texture grit belongs primarily in the dirt footing; building walls must not have photographic grain, tiny all-over scratches, tiny bricks, smooth 3D highlights, or stippled masonry. Keep exterior contours crisp and connected. This is not a clean/pristine rebuilding and not a low-poly/vector graphic.
IMPORTANT effective pixel grain: at final 512x460 canvas, most chips and light/shadow clusters should occupy several whole pixels (roughly 3-8px), outlines generally 1-2px, highlights grouped. Do not add finer detail merely because the exported canvas is larger. Overall object scale stays unchanged. Restrained palette, no blur, no antialiasing or glow.
Canvas/composition: original 512x460 coordinate layout. Keep the same generous empty sky margins; nominal soil line y340 and short ground ending near y460. No long cliff, no other scenery. Output may be 1024x920 exactly double those positions; do not crop tightly or fill the frame with the building. True transparent RGBA exterior and through-openings, NOT a painted checkerboard, black matte or white matte. No labels, text, humans, foreground props or extra buildings. Single isolated BrokenShell sprite only.
