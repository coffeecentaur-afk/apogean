# Maw cave backdrop — provisional study

Independent stretch art branch of the September12 eleven-hour pass. Do not change
ordinary-world routes or promote this art while the user is away. Retain accepted
Wastes scenery and all material/bone study pixels. No new boss, biome tier or lore.

## Direction

Shallow Gullet/ossuary: fractured gray/charcoal geology, dry brown earth, continuous
muted bone supports, hanging ochre fiber, small localized slate membrane and sparse
amber organs. Quiet dark recesses suggest branching caves. A rare swallowed Helix
research remnant can support established lore, not dominate or imply all mutants
are manufactured. Avoid red-meat/purple evil-biome reads, photographed detail,
bright outlines or confusing background bone with actual damaging foreground teeth.
Higher pixel fidelity must not mean enlarged tiny art or micro-detail noise.

## Stages

1. Original opaque environment master: art/layout study only, no fake alpha.
2. Derive separate candidate components using explicit reviewed masks if needed;
   preserve the master, exact kept pixels and independent edge-color audit.
3. Choose and prove the actual engine contract before making a runtime pack.
   Current Engraft underground style assigns four semantic texture slots, NOT
   far/mid/close parallax planes. Its existing placeholder family is not approval.
4. If a larger custom cave compositor is warranted, require bounded QA-only
   lifetime/routing/coverage proof before touching production. Never write beyond
   the public four-slot API, or disguise a flattened concept as tested parallax.

## Required future runtime evidence

1920x1080 and2560x1440 supported viewports; native-scale captures, diagonal camera
travel across all joins, upper/lower cave handoff, no changing landmark placement,
no stretched final row and no exposed asset rectangle. Actual Maw/adjacent-biome
selection must stay independent from a forced laboratory preview. Background is
behind native tiles/walls/entities/UI. Awake/dormant geometry remains identical;
only localized organ illumination changes, never wholesale purification or black
silhouette erasure. Art light cannot stand in for real gameplay tile emission.

Pin source/derived dimensions and hashes; calculate rawColor texel cost separately
from measured process/GPU counters. Load only the scoped study, clear its cached
references on unload and do not dispose engine-shared assets. Final camera factors,
repeat scheme, slots and memory budget remain contracted before runtime coding.
