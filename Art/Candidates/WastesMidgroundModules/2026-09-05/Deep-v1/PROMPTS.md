# Generation prompts — modular landscape study

Built-in image-generation tool; four calls, no API/CLI fallback. Output quality and export properties are measured separately. The source images were not used as finished game assets. Approved local matte processing and deterministic assembly are implemented in `Tools/Preview-WastesModularMid.ps1`.

## Upper hills and cliffs

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: ORIGINAL Wastes midground landscape source for a Terraria-like side-scrolling pixel game, not a finished screenshot.
Input image 1 is the upper-silhouette design reference/edit target. Keep the identities of the broken highway with old pickup, quiet low exposed pipe mound, and abandoned small gas station. Rebuild the canvas into a wide landscape source, target 3072 x 2048. Keep strict straight-on SIDE ELEVATION, absolutely no isometric view.
Main change: these are TOPS OF DEEP GROUNDED HILLS, not floating islands or thin platforms. Below the upper ledges draw deep tall continuous cliff faces, tapering slopes, exposed rock and occasional broken pipe. Terrain descends all the way to the canvas bottom beneath each occupied group; no shallow horizontal cut or pointed floating underside. Two or three large coherent rock planes per cliff, quieter low-contrast shading in the deeper rock, no repeated wavy sediment bands. Upper roads/buildings occupy only the upper third; cliff depth occupies the lower two thirds.
Composition: highway hill at left connects naturally to a broad descending rocky shoulder; a generous EMPTY vertical valley; quiet low pipe hill with a small adjoining eroded rise; a second generous EMPTY vertical valley; station hill at right with its own sloped shoulder. Valleys remain fully open from sky to bottom. Group spacing matters: quiet shoulders, open valley, quiet rise before each new major ruin. Do not connect every hill into a full-width soil strip. Keep clean compatible slopes where shoulders meet their hill. Avoid dense machinery everywhere.
Style: clearly hand-pixeled 2D raster art. Crisp square clusters and stepped outlines. Subdued umber, rust and slate grey, dry ochre grass edges. No realistic painting, no smooth gradients, no noisy micro-speckling, no photorealism, no giant outlined boulders, no bright white dots. Rusting concrete/metal silhouettes readable at Terraria scale. Keep original subjects, no text or logos.
Export backdrop: replace the entire checkerboard with ONE UNIFORM PURE MAGENTA (#FF00FF) authoring matte everywhere outside the terrain and through open holes/valleys, including under the bridge. This matte is intentionally removed locally in the existing approved pipeline. NO CHECKERBOARD, no sky, no shadows or reflections on the magenta, no labels, no UI, no border. Magenta must never occur inside terrain materials. Terrain reaching the image bottom is deliberate coverage, not a visible horizontal base.
```

## Lower cliff continuation

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Image 1 is the source master for three cliff groups on pure magenta. Create a LOWER CLIFF CONTINUATION source only, same wide 3:2 canvas proportions. We are extending these existing cliffs DOWNWARD, not drawing a new landscape. Remove all highway, truck, gas station, topsoil ridges and skyline. Imagine moving the camera downward so the old bottom 250 pixels occupy the new image's top quarter. Maintain three groups in the SAME left/center/right positions and same side boundaries near that top seam, including the two open magenta vertical valleys. Then extend each cliff downward through the rest of the image with freshly drawn natural rocky slopes, stepped eroded faces, one or two quiet exposed pipes and subdued rubble shoulders. All occupied land continues to the bottom; empty valleys stay open from top to bottom, never join the three masses. Broad subdued umber and slate rock planes, not alternating sediment stripes. No floating-island points, no flat visible bases, no repeated/mirrored rows, no rounded boulder pattern. Match the original crisp side-view pixel-art style, native square clusters, subdued browns/greys, sparse ochre, no realistic rendering. Keep the pure #FF00FF background uniform throughout valleys and outside cliffs for our approved matte extraction. No new buildings, foliage, roads, labels, text, UI, checkerboard, or sky. This is a compatible authored extension beneath the original image, not a complete standalone landscape.
```

## Long foreground bank

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create one ORIGINAL foreground terrain-bank source for a 2D side-scrolling post-apocalyptic pixel game. This is foreground scenery behind playable tiles, not a playable floating island. Composition: a SINGLE LONG BROAD gently rolling connected bank, much wider than tall above the soil lip, with weathered dry grass drooping over the edge, a few fallen dead twigs/logs and restrained exposed utility pipes. No standing trees, buildings, gas station, bridge, signs, text, UI, or characters. The lower bulk is deep muted umber/charcoal earth and cracked slate with a few large quiet rock planes, occasional thin sediment seams confined to this foreground. Sparse details; no repeated wavy stripes, no giant white outlined boulders, no noisy bright dots.
The bank must extend downward deeply to the bottom edge. Give its left and right outlines eroded stepped slopes instead of sheer rectangular cut ends. Leave a generous pure-magenta empty margin at BOTH sides all the way down so this bank can sit between open valleys. No pointed floating underside and no shallow platform base. Top land profile in the UPPER QUARTER; deep dark cliff body in the lower three quarters. Wide landscape source, target 2048 x 1536, original crisp pixel clusters without enlargement, straight-on SIDE ELEVATION, no isometric perspective or realistic painting. Restrained palette; warm dry turf, dark brown earth, grey slate. Broad areas of calm material for visual clarity.
Background MUST be uniform PURE #FF00FF magenta authoring matte above/outside the bank and at both side margins, no checkerboard or sky. The matte is intentionally removed through an existing approved local pixel export. Never put magenta into the terrain. Draw one connected long foreground segment, not multiple separate skinny columns.
```

## Foreground cleanup

```text
Edit this ONE foreground bank. Preserve its long connected silhouette, deep downward extent, slope positions, pipes, fallen logs, ground height and canvas framing. Correct ONLY pixel-art finish and alpha edges. Remove ALL red, yellow, magenta and lime halo/fringe pixels around the outer grass, twigs and sides. Empty background must have REAL alpha=0, not black fill and not a checkerboard. Solid material edge pixels must be fully opaque, no soft antialiasing.
Simplify the overly fine grassy hair/noise and shaded rock into deliberate clearly square 2-3 pixel clusters, 3-4 values per material, clean stepped contours, restrained dark umber/charcoal/slate with muted ochre dry turf. No bright white speckles. Straight-on side-view raster pixel art for a Terraria-like game background; no photorealistic texture, airbrush shading, isometric projection or 3D lighting. Retain the broad quiet cliff faces, sparse pipes, long bank and real empty side margins. Do NOT add buildings, trees, lettering, UI, new objects, gradient sky or new sediment stripes. This is a non-destructive cleanup variant of the same bank, not a new composition.
```
