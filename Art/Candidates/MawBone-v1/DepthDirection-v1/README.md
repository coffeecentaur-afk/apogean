# Maw depth materials — proposed, not approved

September 9 user direction: keep the dead grey/black/brown/amber base; explore
secondary colors and other mutations deeper in the Maw. Mutants should differ
in anatomy and host materials rather than all being infection-color swaps.

`concept.png` is an original built-in image-generation direction board. Exact
prompt: `prompt.txt`. SHA256:
`22EFA3BEFA1B51178137304D473726B16F1843F01A92C3CBA07256D5A8CD169D`.
The opaque charcoal backdrop is intentional. Do not infer exact palette count,
native pixels, tile dimensions, lighting values or playable geometry from it.
This is a material close-up, NOT a new entrance design or a scale reference.

Proposed art direction for review:

| Region | Shared foundation | Local variation, not a biome-wide recolor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mouth | Ashy grey-brown bone, dark soil, dirty amber cords | Dry pores, mineralized plates, recognizable host remains |
| Gullet / ossuary chambers | The same bone and earth persist | Cool slate-blue folded membrane; chalky parasite casings; amber glands |
| Stomach / Burning Root | Soot-dark structure with grey still visible | Rust/copper-brown heat-weathering, compressed folds, amber organ pockets |

These materials are proposals, not new ores, enemies, liquid types or unlocks.
The endgame Deep Maw remains its separate later content scope; descending the
pre-Hardmode Gullet does not automatically unlock that endgame ecosystem.
Do not label every wild mutant as a Helix product. Corporate lab designations
remain where lore establishes engineered origins, such as MATRIARCH-7A-1.

Future creatures should carry visible mutation mechanisms: asymmetric limb
growth, parasite burden, altered jaws, skin/scales/fur, fused or mineralized
tissue. Retain enough of an early host to read its origin; later mutations can
become less recognizable. Amber is a linking infection cue, not a required
all-over creature color. No new mob names, stats, behaviors or spawn entries
are approved by this board. PDF creature references remain inspiration only.

Safety/readability invariants: ordinary bone and turf remain non-glowing and
safe; recognizable exposed teeth alone own the brittle contact hazard. Amber
organs/light and optional amber acid retain their separate contracts. Cooler
tissue does not silently introduce a new damage type or imply purification.
The board's small amber pockets are color studies, not a brightness ceiling for
the previously approved strong cave navigation light.
