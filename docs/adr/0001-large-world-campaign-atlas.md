# Require a large world and solve permanent landmarks as one atlas

Apogee's complete campaign initially requires a standard large world and jointly plans the Maw, three permanent Corporate Campuses, guaranteed ruins, vanilla landmarks, and known compatibility exclusions before constructing them. Supporting every world size or placing structures greedily would either force major content omissions or destroy Terraria and third-party world generation; medium compact layouts remain a later target, while critical landmarks fail clearly instead of disappearing silently.

## Consequences

- Players must create a new standard large world for the complete campaign.
- All major structures exist from day one and change access state through progression.
- The generator carries full and compact authored envelopes, reserves them early, and validates the complete combination.
- Optional Outgrowths and minor ruins yield first when space is contested.
- Secret seeds and unsupported world-generation combinations require explicit later validation rather than accidental support claims.
