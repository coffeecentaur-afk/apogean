Choose fixed region versus movable contamination, region survival after mining/purification, damage/contact threshold and grace period, immunity, dormancy behavior, and whether NPCs are affected. Existing [MawAcidCollision](Common/Maw/MawAcidCollision.cs) checks fake acid tiles; its caller is not real-water evidence and its old damage number is not adopted here. Save/sync custom basin state separately through [ModSystem world hooks](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/ModLoader/ModSystem.cs#L322-L377); later edits need explicit replication. All tests below are **required and unrun**, using the intended entrance geometry and actual production draw/damage paths in disposable QA:

- **Contact/locality:** partial fill, feet/head/edge contact, slopes/half-blocks, dry cells, drainage/refill; ordinary neighboring and player-built pools remain safe and visually distinct, including both pools visible together.
- **Movement/transfer:** breach retaining lips; settle, split and reconnect flows; buckets including bottomless placement, sponges and pumps within/across regions; verify the chosen origin policy after every transfer. Test water/lava/honey/shimmer overlaps and resulting solids without deleting unrelated liquid.
- **Presentation/capture:** gameplay and native capture, camera away from player, scene transitions and competing mod biomes; day/night, supported lighting modes, waves, waterfalls, slopes, foreground occlusion and screen edges. Check `-1`, vanilla and valid mod slots; match visual boundaries to harm.
- **Persistence/multiplayer:** save/load the same drained/refilled/modified basins without rebuilding; section boundaries, host plus two clients, dedicated server, late join and reconnect. Exercise concurrent mining/buckets/pumps, differing client scenes, region changes, synchronized harm and no duplicate ticks or stale masks. Dedicated server must not require client draw assets/state.
- **Water/gameplay scope:** swimming, water walking, drowning, fishing and wet projectiles retain intended water behavior; test escape with ordinary ropes/platforms and combined entrance hazards. Record cost under flowing water. Rendering failure keeps acid deferred; compiling or passing mask arithmetic alone cannot promote it.

## Portable-acid follow-up — instant neutralization

**Status:** bounded source assessment only; no runtime edits or live proof. Portable enemy traps outside Maw make the earlier location-only recommendation insufficient. The main task owns the player contract.

**Answer / inference:** instant neutralization removes the need for concentration arithmetic or an origin history, **not the need to distinguish and transport acid identity until contact**. Acid and clean water both remain native WATER; a moving binary tag is the minimum proposed state.

**Supported by the pinned release:**

- Native IDs remain Water=0, Lava=1, Honey=2, Shimmer=3; acid must not claim an extra ID. Native liquid data contains amount/type/update flags, without acid identity. [IDs](https://docs.tmodloader.net/docs/stable/class_liquid_i_d.html), [pinned data layout](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/TileData.Default.cs#L33-L45).
- To inspect unpatched vanilla bodies, this pass read IL from the already-verified [installed assembly](E:/SteamLibrary/steamapps/common/tModLoader/tModLoader.dll), using its shipped Cecil library, without executing the game. This is evidence for [v2026.07.3.0 / 666f699](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/releases/tag/v2026.07.3.0), not a claim that GitHub patch files contain complete vanilla methods.
- `Liquid.Update` directly transfers amount into empty/same-type cells and copies type (downward branch IL_0331–IL_0412); acid and clean water therefore share that branch. `UpdateLiquid` processes updates and can invoke `QuickWater`, which calls `SettleWaterAt`. One ordinary-update interception would not establish coverage of fast settling. [Official method surface](https://docs.tmodloader.net/docs/stable/class_liquid.html); installed bodies above.
- `Wiring.XferWater` directly subtracts/adds amounts and copies native type to empty/same-type outlets; it does not transport custom identity. `Player.ItemCheck_UseBuckets` maps collection/placement to native items/types and has client `sendWater` branches. Pump endpoints and bucket inventory/network transitions need separate treatment; a world-update observer alone does not identify these transfers. Installed bodies above; [pinned wiring entry and server guard](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/Wiring.cs.patch#L129-L133).
- `LiquidCheck`/`GetLiquidMergeTypes` select native solid-producing reactions, including water/lava, water/honey and shimmer contacts under their conditions. These are not a general “adopt the other liquid” operation. Installed bodies above; [official liquid methods](https://docs.tmodloader.net/docs/stable/class_liquid.html).
- Reviewed `ModSystem.Pre/PostUpdateWorld` hooks schedule world work on server/single-player; they supply no source/destination/amount transfer callback. No ready-made provenance hook was established in this pass. [Pinned hook declarations](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/ModLoader/ModSystem.cs#L225-L236).
- `GlobalNPC.UpdateLifeRegen` supports DOT and runs on server **and clients**; the NPC update applies it through native regeneration accounting. Its `damage` argument controls popup size, not an extra hit. [Pinned hook contract](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/ModLoader/GlobalNPC.cs), [pinned call site](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/NPC.cs.patch).

**Consequences / inference:**

- A fixed coordinate flag loses escaped/pumped acid and wrongly acidifies clean refill at the old coordinate. A dynamic coordinate map can work only if transfer/removal logic moves or clears its tags.
- End-of-tick amount differences cannot reliably reconstruct identity: inflow and outflow can cancel, and same-ID mixing can erase the distinction before observation. Neutralization must see pre-contact identity; newly transferred acid must not be mistaken for incoming clean water.
- Clearing the tag is sufficient for acid + plain water. For acid + lava/honey/shimmer, clearing it leaves WATER and native reactions. Adopting the other liquid requires custom type conversion and reaction ordering before native solid creation; post-update recoloring cannot implement that rule.
- Contacted-cell versus whole-connected-pool neutralization, physical contact at partial fills, and simultaneous competing liquids need the main contract. One drop neutralizing an entire connected pool also introduces connected-component work and potentially large liquid conversion.

**Proposed smallest proof, not a supported turnkey implementation:**

- Keep real WATER physics and a sparse acid-tag map. Investigate narrowly scoped, version-checked transfer interception; preserve tags through acid-only movement, clear on actual removal, and neutralize on verified clean-water contact. Full transfer coverage remains unverified.
- Process affected cells/neighbors from observed mutations and known placement/pump endpoints; bound the fixture and queue. No full-world scan. If whole-pool neutralization is selected, measure bounded component traversal and flag invalidation; queue deferral must not permit extra acid damage.
- Prove one explicit acid-carrying container transaction plus one vanilla pump path. Ordinary bucket collection cannot silently become ordinary water and count as successful portability; inventory identity, placement and server validation are part of the proof.
- Save sparse state and send a join baseline plus ordered change/removal updates. Native `NetLiquidModule` serializes coordinates, amount and type, not acid tags (installed body). World-data hooks provide save/join infrastructure, not automatic per-transfer replication. [Pinned persistence/network hooks](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/ModLoader/ModSystem.cs#L322-L377).
- Use server-decided NPC exposure to actual acid-filled volume and one DOT contribution through `UpdateLifeRegen`; mirror state for clients' normal simulation/presentation. Do not add a second strike path or client damage requests. Eligibility/immunity and shared-health segments remain explicit contract/validation cases.

**Required, unrun water/acid fixture:**

1. Outside Maw, use two small isolated pools and a dry receiving trap. Acid must survive falling, spreading, splitting/rejoining and transport; clean water stays safe. Drain/refill must leave no stale acid.
2. Deliver one nonzero native amount unit (`LiquidAmount=1`, 1/255 cell) of clean water into acid and acid into clean water. Record contact before native settling can discard/round that unit; neutralization must match the main contract, clear harm/presentation together, and leave an unconnected acid pool intact.
3. Exercise the container, pump, ordinary/bottomless-water placement and sponge removal within the bounded fixture; log transfer order and compare native amounts with a control to detect additional liquid loss/duplication. Passing ordinary flow does not certify fast settling.
4. Repeat on a dedicated server with two remote clients, concurrent transfers, late join and save/reload. Verify identical acid state and one NPC health-loss rate regardless of observer count, with one death/loot resolution; include partial contact and a shared-health NPC.
5. After water/acid passes, test each other liquid separately against the now-selected rule: remove acidity, then allow native reactions. Verify resulting solids/liquids and untouched neighboring liquids. Selection of this rule does not certify its implementation.

**Unverified / stop boundary:** interception completeness, exact one-drop timing, neutralization/reaction ordering and multiplayer ordering are engineering proof gates. The source supports a binary-metadata candidate, neither an off-the-shelf portable-acid hook nor an impossibility verdict. No further broad survey is needed before that bounded proof.
