# Maw entrance basins: real-water revisit — 2026-09-08

Status: source-verified feasibility research; no implementation, build, game test, or evidence promotion. Scope is the yellow/orange acid WATER beside the tooth-lined Gullet in the newest sketch. Teeth belong to the main design intake. The supersession banner in [the old liquid report](RESEARCH_MAW_ACID_LIQUIDS.md) already rejects its historical fake-tile selection; that selection and its test expectations are not current authority. Follow [the September 8 intake](MAW_SKETCH_DIRECTION_2026-09-08.md).

**Recommendation:** investigate one real-water basin with a saved spatial acid region and matching local presentation. Ordinary water physics plus location-based harm is feasible. Basin-only yellow/orange rendering is a separate, unverified integration problem; `ModWaterStyle` alone cannot provide it. Propose that water is hazardous only while inside the region, regardless of origin. This policy needs a design decision: it does not preserve acid after escape. If the visual probe fails or transported acid is required, keep acid deferred rather than restoring fake blocks.

## Local rendering and capture

**Feasible avenue, unverified result:** draw a liquid-aware local tint/foam/surface layer, or investigate a narrowly scoped renderer hook. A supported `PostDrawTiles` overlay exists, but its existence does not prove correct liquid occlusion, slopes, waterfalls, lighting modes or capture. Follow changing water coverage; never leave a painted full basin after drainage. Do not promise exact recoloring from an additive overlay. [Overlay hook](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/ModLoader/ModSystem.cs#L305-L309).

Calamity's public mirror at `1a8cebd27ec5615316b78f71973446b5528d2b78` separates [biome water selection](https://github.com/CalamityTeam/CalamityModPublic/blob/1a8cebd27ec5615316b78f71973446b5528d2b78/BiomeManagers/SulphurousSeaBiome.cs#L15-L20) from [regional underwater poisoning](https://github.com/CalamityTeam/CalamityModPublic/blob/1a8cebd27ec5615316b78f71973446b5528d2b78/CalPlayer/CalamityPlayerLifeRegen.cs#L166-L190). Its coordinate-aware color effects use its own interfaces and [IL/detour renderer integration](https://github.com/CalamityTeam/CalamityModPublic/blob/1a8cebd27ec5615316b78f71973446b5528d2b78/Systems/Graphic/LiquidSystem/SpecialLiquidDrawingSystem.cs#L18-L74), including normal liquids, partial liquids, old water drawing and waterfalls. This is evidence of additional engineering, not a standard tML basin API or proof of transported acid. No dependency or source reuse is proposed.

**Capture risk is concrete.** [EngraftBiome's comment](Content/Biomes/EngraftBiome.cs) records the `-1` crash. Current official capture code can construct a capture biome from a modded surface background while forwarding an unchecked scene water value; this supports the suspected path, but the crash was not reproduced here. [Capture source](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/Graphics/Capture/CaptureBiome.cs.patch#L22-L31). Also, [TileLabPlayer.RunCaptureProbe](Content/Diagnostics/TileLabPlayer.cs) accepts only water slots below `Main.maxLiquidTypes`; it would discard valid mod slots registered above that boundary. Future validation must distinguish loaded mod slots from `-1`, and prove the acid's actual draw path. A fallback-water screenshot is not acid-render proof.

## Open choices and required proof

Choose fixed region versus movable contamination, region survival after mining/purification, damage/contact threshold and grace period, immunity, dormancy behavior, and whether NPCs are affected. Existing [MawAcidCollision](Common/Maw/MawAcidCollision.cs) checks fake acid tiles; its caller is not real-water evidence and its old damage number is not adopted here. Save/sync custom basin state separately through [ModSystem world hooks](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/ModLoader/ModSystem.cs#L322-L377); later edits need explicit replication. [UpdateBadLifeRegen](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/ModLoader/ModPlayer.cs#L192-L198) supports damage-over-time but runs on multiple peers: define ownership and prevent duplicate damage.

All tests below are **required and unrun**, using the intended entrance geometry and actual production draw/damage paths in disposable QA:

- **Contact/locality:** partial fill, feet/head/edge contact, slopes/half-blocks, dry cells, drainage/refill; ordinary neighboring and player-built pools remain safe and visually distinct, including both pools visible together.
- **Movement/transfer:** breach retaining lips; settle, split and reconnect flows; buckets including bottomless placement, sponges and pumps within/across regions; verify the chosen origin policy after every transfer. Test water/lava/honey/shimmer overlaps and resulting solids without deleting unrelated liquid.
- **Presentation/capture:** gameplay and native capture, camera away from player, scene transitions and competing mod biomes; day/night, supported lighting modes, waves, waterfalls, slopes, foreground occlusion and screen edges. Check `-1`, vanilla and valid mod slots; match visual boundaries to harm.
- **Persistence/multiplayer:** save/load the same drained/refilled/modified basins without rebuilding; section boundaries, host plus two clients, dedicated server, late join and reconnect. Exercise concurrent mining/buckets/pumps, differing client scenes, region changes, synchronized harm and no duplicate ticks or stale masks. Dedicated server must not require client draw assets/state.
- **Water/gameplay scope:** swimming, water walking, drowning, fishing and wet projectiles retain intended water behavior; test escape with ordinary ropes/platforms and combined entrance hazards. Record cost under flowing water. Rendering failure keeps acid deferred; compiling or passing mask arithmetic alone cannot promote it.
