# Maw activity — same landscape, sleeping or awake

September 7, 2026: user approved the reclaimed-city vegetation style and proposed
the same layered-state method for Maw dormancy and awakening. This records a
future visual family, not new runtime implementation or a change in development order.

## Confirmed method and existing lore

- Preserve each Maw composition's terrain, bones, ruins, roots, depth coverage,
  placement and camera anchors. Change registered biological detail/light layers,
  not the whole panorama. Reuse the authoring/export/validation method, not the
  Wastes city artwork or its proposed restoration curves.
- Dormancy follows MATRIARCH-7A-1's defeat; Wall of Flesh/Hardmode awakens the
  network again, as already recorded in the Bible. Do not invent new triggers,
  a day/night sleep cycle or additional boss progression from this art decision.
- Dormant Maw is still Maw: dimmer amber organs and minimal biological activity,
  not green plants, purification, removed terrain or zero intrinsic spread.
- Wastes reclamation and Maw activity are separate inputs. A low activity level
  cannot grant restoration credit or change a tile's conversion state. Neither
  visual effect owns encounter difficulty, spread rates or quest progression.

## Proposed visual treatment — preview before implementation

| State | Organ/light treatment | Biological motion |
|---|---|---|
| Dormant | Subdued amber in the same glands/cords; retain navigation readability. | Faint slow pulses and sparse ambient activity. |
| Awakening | Registered amber channels brighten gradually without moving terrain. | Increasing pulse/motion; avoid a screen-wide flash. |
| Awake | Stronger localized yellow-amber organs, quiet non-glowing soil/bone. | Readable breathing/pulses and restrained spore activity, not a particle curtain. |

These cues are recommendations, not approval of unmade art, exact animation
timings or brightness values. Never make ordinary turf glow or turn dormancy into
an unreadably black cave. Decorative background emission and actual terrain
navigation lighting need separate contracts; painted glow does not implement light.

Keep the base artwork separate from reviewed detail, silhouette and emission
masks. Any moving detail must remain attached across all frames. Use one stable
world progression input shared with clients; camera distance, biome transitions
and day/night lighting remain independent presentation concerns. Save/reload and
multiplayer behavior require proof before this becomes a gameplay-facing system.

and shallow-Maw sequence. This idea does not authorize jumping into a full Maw
