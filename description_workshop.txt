[h1]The Apogee Wastes[/h1]
Two hundred years ago a corporate bio-arms race ended the world. Now three
mega-corporations — an arms manufacturer running a war with no enemy left, a
biotech giant still iterating on the bioweapon that broke containment, and a
security AI enforcing martial law on a world with no one left to protect —
along with a mutant horde finally learning to coordinate, are all that's left
running things. You answer to none of them. Yet.
