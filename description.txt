Apogean: The Apogee Wastes is a post-apocalyptic sci-fi overhaul. A corporate
bio-arms race ended the world two hundred years ago, and now three
mega-corporations and a rising mutant horde run what's left of it. Fight
through gene-spliced monstrosities, rogue security AI, and salvaged war
machines to carve out your own territory in the Wastes.
