## 1. Foreground grass, exposed faces, and background walls

**Verified — tile and wall are separate data layers.** Each coordinate has a `TileType` and a `WallType`; `HasTile` describes foreground occupancy, and a wall may exist without a foreground tile. Tile and wall frame coordinates are separate too. Thus a vertical exposed face of a foreground block is not a Terraria background wall. [Pinned Tile API](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/Tile.TML.cs).

**Inference for the proposal:** fiber on a floor, cave ceiling, or vertical rock face can be represented as a grass-like foreground terrain variant with edge art. Fiber painted across the cave's background wall requires a separate `ModWall` or wall drawing/conversion policy. Ordinary tile identity is per cell, not an independent material slot on every face; independently persistent face coatings or coatings over multiple preserved substrates would need an explicitly chosen representation. Do not silently make “side-face fiber” mean “replace the background wall.” [Tile API](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/Tile.TML.cs).

**Verified — framing and growth are distinct.** The stable framing patch handles grass through `TileID.Sets.Grass`, `GrassSpecial`, and `NeedsGrassFraming`, reads `NeedsGrassFramingDirt`, and evaluates neighbors above, below, left, right, and diagonally. This establishes directional framing machinery, not a complete spreading-material implementation. `RandomUpdate` is the documented growth/spread hook shared by mod tiles and walls, with separate tile/wall dispatch. [Grass framing source](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/WorldGen.cs.patch), [shared hook](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/ModLoader/ModBlockType.cs), [wall dispatch](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/ModLoader/WallLoader.cs).

**Verified — a helper exists, but its full behavior was not verified here.** Official stable docs expose `WorldGen.SpreadGrass` with coordinates, source dirt type, destination grass type, a repeat option, and `TileColorCache`. Its entry does not explain every eligibility rule. Do not infer exact light/depth/liquid restrictions, recursion limits, exposure tests, or multiplayer synchronization merely from that signature. `SpreadInfectionToNearbyTile` is separately documented as hardmode biome infection, with hardmode/Journey/protection rules; those are not automatically the right semantics for ecological Maw fiber. [WorldGen API](https://docs.tmodloader.net/docs/stable/class_world_gen.html).

**Verified limitation:** the official `ExampleGrass` request #4507 remains open (last updated 2025-11-03). It calls out mining transformations, block replacement, mowing, flowers, and related parity issues; its listed detours are reported candidates, not an endorsed mandatory recipe. The pinned ExampleMod tree has an `ExampleVine`, whose anchor is still `ExampleBlock` with a TODO for eventual grass. No complete current ExampleGrass recipe was established. [Official issue](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/issues/4507), [pinned example](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/ExampleMod/Content/Tiles/Plants/ExampleVine.cs).

**Inference:** the all-face fiber visual is feasible using native framing plus original art, but the specification must separately define eligible substrates, exposed-face detection, growth boundaries/rate, underground behavior, and what mining or cleansing restores. A grass flag alone does not establish those semantics. Bone ribs and teeth should have explicit conversion eligibility rather than being incidentally swallowed by a general spread rule.

## 2. Fibrous vines: established behavior and directional limits

**Verified in ExampleVine:** a `GlobalTile.RandomUpdate` handles both spawning from its anchor and extending existing tips. The example restricts growth above the surface, checks actuation, vine density, empty space below, lava, and an anchor within a bounded upward search; it rejects bottom slopes. It copies paint/coating, reframes, and sends a tile-square update when on the server. These are example choices, not universal constraints that Maw must inherit. Its framing logic also handles changed/removed anchors and conversion between vine types. [Pinned ExampleVine](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/ExampleMod/Content/Tiles/Plants/ExampleVine.cs).

**Verified drawing contract:** `IsVine` participates in density/conversion behavior; `VineThreads` enables wind/player-interaction drawing and requires root registration in `PreDraw`. The example calls the root helper with `(j, i)` and returns false to suppress duplicate ordinary drawing. `ReverseVineThreads` covers upward vines, with a different root helper, and is used by vanilla seaweed. Neither documented set promises sideways crawling vines. [TileID source documentation](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/ID/TileID.cs.patch), [example drawing](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/ExampleMod/Content/Tiles/Plants/ExampleVine.cs).

**Inference:** ceiling-hung strands have a close official implementation reference. Floor-growing fibers can investigate reverse-vine drawing, while growth and anchors still need their own rules. Fibers crawling along side faces or turning around corners require custom directional growth/attachment logic or decorative edge rendering; simply rotating ExampleVine art does not establish that behavior. Whether those fibers occupy traversable space or exist only as decoration is a specification decision.

## 3. Native merging versus texture transition overlays

| Mechanism | Verified behavior | Consequence for Maw |
|---|---|---|
| Foreground native merging | `Main.tileMergeDirt` declares dirt merge frames. The official custom-framing example uses `ChecksForMerge`, `ModifyFrameMerge`, and `TileMergeAttempt` for snow, plus an explicit snow-to-custom-tile entry in `Main.tileMerge`. | Both sides of a boundary need coherent framing. A merge relationship cannot supply missing atlas art. [ExampleBlock](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/ExampleMod/Content/Tiles/ExampleBlock.cs), [ExampleCustomFramingTile](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/ExampleMod/Content/Tiles/ExampleCustomFramingTile.cs). |
| Background-wall blending | `Main.wallBlend` groups wall types so their inter-wall black border is omitted. ExampleWallAdvanced demonstrates it against CogWall. | Wall blending is separate from foreground tile merging and does not generate a fibrous texture transition. [Main documentation source](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/patches/tModLoader/Terraria/Main.cs.patch), [wall example](https://github.com/tModLoader/tModLoader/blob/666f69962d3bdffde54fc14025f02634965b4e7c/ExampleMod/Content/Walls/ExampleWallAdvanced.cs). |
| Custom transition overlay | `PreDraw`/`PostDraw` support extra drawing around tiles/walls; `PostDraw` has explicit invisible-tile handling caveats. | **Inference:** an authored fiber fringe or material-transition mask can use these hooks, but requires owned art, neighbor selection, lighting/paint/coating handling, and correct draw order. This is extra renderer work, not a benefit automatically supplied by a merge boolean. [ModBlockType drawing API](https://docs.tmodloader.net/docs/stable/class_mod_block_type.html). |

**Verified:** `ModifyFrameMerge` changes adjacent merge values before vanilla chooses atlas frame coordinates; it only runs if `TileFrame` returns true. Its `-2` merge convention selects custom transition frames when the appropriate set is enabled. This is frame selection, not arbitrary interpolation between two materials' textures. [ModTile API](https://docs.tmodloader.net/docs/stable/class_mod_tile.html).

**Inference for readability:** selectively join fiber and its substrate while retaining a deliberate bone boundary and an identifiable tooth silhouette. A connected, hard-alpha pixel fringe can make the transition less abrupt without softening the approved brittle art. Neither native blending nor a lighting mod establishes collision or damage. Terraria does expose tile contact-damage sets used by spikes/thorns/cactus, so hazards are separately implementable; exact tooth orientation and hit coverage remain design/test work. [Contact-damage documentation](https://docs.tmodloader.net/docs/stable/class_tile_i_d_1_1_sets.html).

## 4. Exact named mods and compatibility limits

### Better Blending / “Better Blends”

**Verified identity:** **Better Blending**, internal name **`BetterBlending`**, author **Orion**, Workshop **2816941454**. It is tagged Client and tML 1.4.3/1.4.4. The author's description says it changes environmental tile blend relationships and offers an All-Blend option; its ordinary behavior preserves building-block combinations. **“Better Blends” remains an unresolved supplied name:** no separate author-owned project under that exact title was verified. Treat Better Blending as the identified candidate, not a proven alias for another project. [Author Workshop page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2816941454&l=english).

**Verified author changelog:** latest listed version **1.2.6**, 2025-02-25, reports an ore-generation fix. Version **1.2.4** limits All-Blend to solid, non-frame-important tiles, explicitly excluding vines and furniture. Version **1.2.1** describes resetting all tile/dirt blending data on unload to support All-Blend; **1.2.3** reports an attempted fix for stale mod tile IDs after cancelled loading. These entries establish relevant global-data interactions and eligibility limits. They do not certify those fixes on the target 2026 tML build. [Author change notes](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2816941454).

**Unresolved:** no verified public author source repository, per-tile opt-out/API contract, or Apogean compatibility guarantee was found in this pass. The author evidence supports blend-data changes; it does not establish a texture-transition-overlay service. **Inference:** solid Maw terrain could interact with All-Blend, potentially erasing intended material boundaries; test both config modes and load/unload before making any compatibility claim. The documented exclusion means it should not be relied on to join non-solid fibrous vines.

## 5. Integration advice and unresolved live-test items

**Research recommendation, not an implemented dependency decision:** retain native tML growth, framing, collision, and readable owned art as the baseline. Treat Better Blending and Lights And Shadows as optional visual compatibility targets. Their stated purposes and the official native APIs establish no requirement to depend on either for fiber spread, vines, bone structure, or teeth hazards. Do not let client visual options determine authoritative growth or damage.

The main agent should retain these acceptance questions for a later authorized fixture/test pass; none was run here:

1. **Fiber growth:** floor, ceiling, and both side faces; diagonal-only contact; exposed versus enclosed cells; allowed substrates; underground depth; liquid contact; bounded spread; mining/cleansing restoration; player-built exclusions and save/reload. Verify the actual `SpreadGrass` behavior before choosing it over explicit growth logic.
2. **Vines:** downward, upward, and any requested sideways variants; cut/removed/converted/actuated anchors; slopes and half blocks; density/length caps; paint/coatings; server/client convergence and chain removal.
3. **Material transitions:** both directions of every intended fiber/substrate/bone boundary, inner/outer corners, slopes, half blocks, and wall-only regions. Ensure teeth remain visually and mechanically distinct. Verify overlay visibility under Echo Coating and variable lighting.
4. **Optional-mod matrix:** native baseline; Better Blending ordinary settings; All-Blend; Lights alone; both together. Record exact package versions/configs. Include cancelled load, unload/reload, existing/new world, ore generation, and frame refresh after edits because of the author's documented blend-data history.
5. **Renderer interaction:** lighting/bloom toggles, forced lighting/wave settings, day/night and surface/underground views, window/resolution/zoom changes, native camera capture and return to play, custom tile/wall overlays, and differing client visual settings. Measure performance on the intended setup rather than inferring it from source.

No broad mod comparison or art/code adoption is implied. The evidence supports pursuing the proposed material roles with explicit foreground/wall and growth/rendering separation; final feasibility and compatibility remain conditional on those tests.
